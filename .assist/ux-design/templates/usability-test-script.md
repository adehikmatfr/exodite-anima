## 1. Introduction and Consent (read aloud)

"Thank you for joining. We are testing the <product>, not you; there are no wrong answers. Please think aloud. I may not answer questions during tasks, because I want to see what you would do on your own. You can stop at any time without giving a reason."

Consent checklist (record verbally on the recording, or on the form):
- [ ] Purpose explained; participation voluntary
- [ ] Recording of screen and voice agreed (yes / no); who will see it; retention period
- [ ] Only pseudonym (P##) used in reports; right to withdraw and to have data deleted
- [ ] Incentive not dependent on performance
- [ ] Recording started only after consent

## 2. Warm-up Questions

1. <Role or context question, for example: "How do you currently handle <task>?">
2. <Frequency and tools used>
3. <Anything relevant to screening, confirm only>

## 3. Tasks

Give each task as a scenario on a card, without interface words. Order: easiest first or counterbalanced; note the order used.

| # | Scenario (what participant reads) | Success criteria (observable) | Time limit | Max assists | Linked `FEAT-` |
|---|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------|------------|-------------|----------------|
| T1 | "<You want to ... >" | <reaches screen X and completes Y without error> | <3 min> | <1> | <FEAT-NNN> |
| T2 | <...> | <...> | <...> | <...> | <...> |

Task outcome codes: Success (unaided), Success with assist, Partial, Fail, Abandoned, Timed out.

## 4. Probes (use only after the task or a natural pause)

- "What were you expecting to happen?"
- "What made you choose that?"
- "You paused there. What were you thinking?"
- "How would you describe this screen to a colleague?"
Avoid: "Was that easy?", "Do you like it?", and any hint of the correct path.

## 5. Post-task Questions

After each task: Single Ease Question, "Overall, how difficult or easy was this task?" (1 very difficult to 7 very easy); one open question: "What, if anything, was confusing?"

## 6. Final Questions

1. <What was the best and worst part?>
2. <What would you change first?>
3. SUS questionnaire (10 items, 1-5 scale) (optional for fewer than 5 participants; scores are unstable)
4. Anything else you want to tell us?

Close: thank, explain incentive and data retention, stop recording.

## 7. Observer Ground Rules (optional)

Observers stay muted and off camera, write notes only in the table below, and send questions to the moderator through the back channel. No screenshots of participants.
