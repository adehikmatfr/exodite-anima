## Context
Six owner decisions were still open after the specifications were answered. Options were offered by the assistant; the owner chose as below.

## Decisions
| # | Question | Decision | Consequence |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | How is iOS built and tested? | **Android first, iOS follows** when a Mac or cloud CI is available. | The first store release is Android only. iOS stays unverified (RISK-004 open); the iOS backup exclusion and privacy manifest are not built. Store and rollout wording must not promise iOS. |
| 2 | Reference device for the performance targets | **An emulator with limited resources.** | The assistant's evidence: emulator graphics speed changed between sessions on identical code (TC-019: 80% then 7% to 14%; a plain list gave 0%), so emulator rendering numbers cannot prove the 95% frames target. CPU-bound numbers (key derivation, search, open time) are usable as indications. The provisional targets in `nfr-targets-v1` therefore stay provisional, and TC-019, TC-045, TC-014 and TC-067 cannot be marked passed until a physical phone is measured. The owner may reverse this at any time by naming a phone. |
| 3 | High-contrast mode in v1? | **No.** Light and dark themes are enough. | Design review finding N2 is closed as deferred; revisit if users ask. The 17 checked colour pairs already meet WCAG 2.2 AA. |
| 4 | Icon set | **Replace Flutter's default Material Icons with a more neutral set.** Chosen 2026-09-20: **Lucide** (ISC). | Done 2026-09-20: the 8 icons in use were swapped to Lucide and registered in the asset register. Bundled as the official icon font, not through a third-party Flutter package (see `dependency-review.md`). |
| 5 | Welcome screen at 200% text | **The main button stays pinned at the bottom**; the content above it scrolls. | Design review finding N4 is closed. The build already pins its action buttons; a test at 200% text confirms it (`app/integration_test/feat003_004_ui_test.dart`). |
| 6 | Recruiting for the usability study (RS-001) | **Friends and acquaintances, no payment.** | 5 to 6 people, recorded only as P01, P02 and so on. Known bias toward people the owner knows; the report must say so, and at least one participant using a screen reader or large text is still needed by the protocol. No incentive, so no budget line. |

## Consequences
- Updates `status.md` (pending list), the design review record, RS-001 section 5, and the release plan.
- Open follow-ups: pick and review the icon set; check the name exodite-anima on the stores; write the legal self-assessment.
