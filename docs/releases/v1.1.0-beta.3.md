# Exodite Anima v1.1.0-beta.3 (pre-release, Android)

A free journal that keeps everything on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the app asks for no internet permission. This is an early version for testers, not a finished release.

## What's fixed since v1.1.0-beta.2
- **Adding a photo to a brand-new entry did nothing.** If you tapped "Add photo" before writing anything, neither the camera nor your photo library opened. Fixed: the app now tells you to write something first, since a photo needs a saved entry to attach to.
- **A photo could vanish right after you took or picked it.** Opening the camera or your photo library sends the app to the background for a moment, which used to trigger the app's "lock immediately" rule before the photo could be saved - so it was lost, and you landed back on the list asking for your passcode again. Fixed: the app now finishes saving the photo first, then locks. You'll still be asked for your passcode when you come back, same as before - the photo just won't disappear anymore.
- **The camera permission is now requested properly**, at the moment you choose "Take a photo," with a clear reason. No new permission was added for the photo library, since Android's own picker needs none.

No other behaviour changed.

## Which file
- `exodite-anima-v1.1.0-beta.3-arm64-v8a.apk`: most phones made in the last several years.
- `exodite-anima-v1.1.0-beta.3-armeabi-v7a.apk`: older 32-bit phones.
- Android 7.0 or newer. iOS is not available.

## Install
1. Download the file for your phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from this source; allow it for this install only.
2. Already on an earlier beta? This update is signed with the same key, so it installs over it without losing your journal.
3. Optional check: `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of each file. The signing certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint is `1E:B2:78:FB:7D:62:A2:1F:ED:3E:95:36:4B:75:41:54:BB:88:72:88:E7:E1:54:3F:FE:F4:9B:93:A0:78:92:E4`, the same as every earlier release. Updates will be signed with the same key.

## Please read
- **A forgotten passcode cannot be recovered.** Nobody can reset it. Use Settings, Export your journal, from time to time and keep the file somewhere you control.
- Photos keep their original embedded metadata (for example, location data a phone camera may record) as-is: nothing is stripped or asked about in this version. Keep that in mind if you share an exported photo with someone else.
- Not reviewed by an independent security reviewer yet. This release makes one deliberate, bounded change to when the app locks: while a photo pick from the camera or library is in progress, the app waits until it finishes before locking, so the photo is not lost. The screen is still hidden from the app-switcher and from screenshots throughout.
- Tested at the unit/widget/format level, including an automated test of this exact fix; the real camera and photo-library pick itself is still not exercised by an automated test, so please try adding a photo (to both a new and an existing entry) on your phone and let us know how it goes.
- Opening with the passcode takes a moment on purpose (it is what protects your journal); it depends on the phone.
- Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app on purpose, including over a photo.
