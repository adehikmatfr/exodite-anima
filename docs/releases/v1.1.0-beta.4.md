# Exodite Anima v1.1.0-beta.4 (pre-release, Android)

A free journal that keeps everything on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the app asks for no internet permission. This is an early version for testers, not a finished release.

## What's fixed since v1.1.0-beta.3
- **The text you type could be invisible.** In the editor, the mood row, tags and photos took up so much room that, with the keyboard open, the writing area shrank to nothing and what you typed could not be seen. Fixed: everything under the date bar now scrolls together, and the writing area always keeps room for several lines. Tapping the empty space under your text no longer starts typing; tap on the text itself.
- **A photo could bring back a second copy of your entry.** Adding a photo to a new entry saves it quietly first; the app then kept a leftover draft that, after the app closed or locked, was offered back as a new entry without the photo. Fixed: that draft is no longer kept.
- **The mood row was too wide on phones 360 dp wide** (a common size), so the last mood could be cut off. Fixed: the five moods now share the width evenly.

No other behaviour changed.

## Which file
- `exodite-anima-v1.1.0-beta.4-arm64-v8a.apk`: most phones made in the last several years.
- `exodite-anima-v1.1.0-beta.4-armeabi-v7a.apk`: older 32-bit phones.
- Android 7.0 or newer. iOS is not available.

## Install
1. Download the file for your phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from this source; allow it for this install only.
2. Already on an earlier beta? This update is signed with the same key, so it installs over it without losing your journal.
3. Optional check: `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of each file. The signing certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint is `1E:B2:78:FB:7D:62:A2:1F:ED:3E:95:36:4B:75:41:54:BB:88:72:88:E7:E1:54:3F:FE:F4:9B:93:A0:78:92:E4`, the same as every earlier release. Updates will be signed with the same key.

## Please read
- **A forgotten passcode cannot be recovered.** Nobody can reset it. Use Settings, Export your journal, from time to time and keep the file somewhere you control.
- Photos keep their original embedded metadata (for example, location data a phone camera may record) as-is: nothing is stripped or asked about in this version. Keep that in mind if you share an exported photo with someone else.
- Not reviewed by an independent security reviewer yet. Since v1.1.0-beta.3, while a photo pick from the camera or library is in progress, the app waits until it finishes before locking, so the photo is not lost. The screen is still hidden from the app-switcher and from screenshots throughout.
- Tested at the unit/widget/format level, including automated tests of the first two fixes, and tried on an Android emulator (typing, and taking a photo with the camera). Not yet tried on a real phone, and the photo-library pick and adding a photo to an existing entry were not tried, so please try those and let us know how it goes.
- Opening with the passcode takes a moment on purpose (it is what protects your journal); it depends on the phone.
- Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app on purpose, including over a photo.
