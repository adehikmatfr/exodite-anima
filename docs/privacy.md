# Privacy statement

**Draft, reviewed and accepted by the app owner on 2026-09-23. Still to be reconciled with the final release build before it is linked from a store listing.**

Exodite Anima is a private journal. Here is exactly what that means.

## The short version

- Your writing never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it.
- There is no account, no sign-up, and no server. Nobody but you can read your journal.
- The app does not use the internet. It has no permission to.
- Nothing you write is collected, analysed, or shared. There is no advertising and no analytics.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you write is kept in an encrypted file on your phone, unlocked by your passcode (and, if you turn it on, your face or fingerprint, which your phone checks — the app never receives it). Nobody, including the people who made this app, can read your journal: there is no way for us to see it, and no way to reset your passcode and recover it if you forget it. That is the trade: total privacy, with no safety net but the one described below.

## The one safety net: export

Because there is no account and no cloud backup, if you lose your phone or delete the app, your journal is gone unless you exported it first. Export makes a file — encrypted by default, or in plain text if you choose, with a clear warning when you do — that you save wherever you like. Import restores it fully, on this phone or a new one. The app reminds you if it has been a while since your last export.

## What the app asks permission for

Only one thing: to check your face or fingerprint to unlock the app, if you turn that on. Your phone's operating system does that check; the app is only told yes or no. Everything else — camera, contacts, location, notifications, network — is either not requested at all, or (for photos and audio, planned for a later version) will only be requested when you actually use that feature, with its own explanation at the time.

## What never happens

- Your entries, your passcode, and your export password are never sent anywhere.
- Nothing you write appears in a log, a crash report, or a notification.
- The app is excluded from your phone's automatic cloud backup, so a cloud backup of your phone does not include your journal.
- Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked or hidden while an entry is open (Android; iOS support is planned).

## Legal notes

This app has no server, collects nothing, and shares nothing, which is why it is not subject to data-protection laws that regulate companies handling other people's data (an engineering reading, not legal advice; the full assessment is in the project's own records). App store forms are answered "no data collected, no data shared" on that same basis.

## If something changes

If a future version ever needs to send anything off your phone (which is not planned), this statement and the relevant store forms will be updated first, and you will see what changed in the release notes before you update.

## Questions

This app has no support server or account, so there is no dashboard to check. Open an issue in the project's public repository, or read the export format specification (planned) if you want to read your data outside the app.
